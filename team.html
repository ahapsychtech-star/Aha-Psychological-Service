About Team

Our Team

Moges G/Mariam

Title: – Director, Senior Counselor and Trainer

Mr. Moges G/Mariam is a counseling psychologist by profession who holds an MA degree in Counseling Psychology and a BA degree in Educational Psychology. He also has another master’s degree in Positive and Cross-Cultural Psychotherapy.

He has ample experience working for more than 20 years in various organizations, including the Ministry of Education, Christian Relief Development Association (CRDA), Family Guidance Association of Ethiopia (FGAE), and John Hopkins University Center for Communication Programs (JHU/CCP) Ethiopia field office, where he held different capacities.

Mr. Moges has managed numerous large-scale and small-scale project evaluations, feasibility studies, behavioral interventions, manual development, and trainings. He is the founder and director of Aha Psychological Services.

Dr. Alebachew Alemnew

Title: – Manager and Researcher

Dr. Alebachew earned his PhD in Education from Tezpur Central University in India. He also holds an MA in Counseling Psychology and a Bachelor of Education in Pedagogical Science and Composite English, as well as a diploma in Adult Education. Additionally, he has completed several trainings on project management, result-oriented project planning, instructional skills, research methodology, girl power program studies, and more.

Dr. Alebachew has experience in action research, formative assessment, baseline surveys and large-scale project and program evaluations. He has worked in government, non-government, private organizations, and universities for about 20 years as a program manager, instructor, and trainer. He has managed numerous research projects, led  program and project evaluations, and developed training manuals in the fields of education , life skills and psychology.

Dr. Alebachew is a co-founder and Manager of Aha Psychological Services and leads the research and consultancy unit of the company.

Abel Woldemichael

Mr. Abel is a PhD candidate in Counseling Psychology at Addis Ababa University. He holds a BA in Psychology and an MA in Clinical Psychology, both of which were earned from the Addis Ababa University School of Psychology.
